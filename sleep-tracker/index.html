Private by design

Recording and analysis happen on your iPhone or iPad. No account is needed, and your audio, detected events, and sleep scores are never uploaded to our servers or shared with advertisers. Delete individual nights or all data whenever you like.

Free and Premium

Sleep Tracker is free to download with ads. An optional one-time Lifetime purchase removes ads and unlocks the full report archive, trends, playback, and export features.

Good to know

Sleep Tracker is for personal awareness, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Detection depends on phone placement, background noise, and hardware. Requires microphone access; recording overnight works best with the device charging. Available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.
